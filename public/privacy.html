Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Antortiq Technologies ("Antortiq", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use our platform — including the Antortiq Order Panel, WhatsApp automation, email communication, pixel tracking, and related APIs.

We operate as a data processor for the brands and merchants ("Merchants") who use our services, and as a data fiduciary for data we collect directly (such as when you browse our website or contact us). This policy covers both roles.

By using Antortiq's services, you acknowledge you have read and understood this policy. If you are a customer of an Antortiq-powered brand, please also review that brand's own privacy notice.

Who We Are

Antortiq Technologies is a technology company registered in India, providing post-purchase automation and D2C fulfilment infrastructure to fashion and lifestyle brands. Our registered address and contact details are listed in the Contact Us section below.

We are a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for data collected via our own channels, and a Data Processor for personal data processed on behalf of our Merchant clients.

How We Use It

  • Order management: display, filter, stage-update, and export orders in the Antortiq Panel on behalf of Merchants.
  • WhatsApp automation: send order confirmation, shipment tracking, NDR (non-delivery report), and return/exchange notifications to end customers at Merchant instruction.
  • Email communication: send branded transactional emails (shipped, out for delivery, return initiated) and demo sequences when requested via the Panel.
  • Pixel analytics: aggregate storefront event data into funnel metrics (view → cart → checkout → purchase) surfaced to the Merchant. No individual customer profiles are built or sold.
  • Abandon cart recovery: send WhatsApp or email re-engagement messages to customers who left items in their cart, on Merchant instruction and using coupons the Merchant sets.
  • Logistics coordination: update shipment stages, trigger cron-based tracking pings to courier APIs, and surface tracking data in the Panel.
  • Platform improvement: anonymised, aggregated usage patterns to improve product reliability, response time, and feature design. No individual behaviour is profiled.
  • Legal compliance: retain records as required by applicable Indian law, including GST invoice data.

We process personal data only on documented instruction from the Merchant or as strictly necessary to provide the subscribed service.

Retention

We retain data for the period necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations:

  • Order & shipping data: retained for 7 years from the order date to satisfy GST record-keeping requirements under the CGST Act, 2017.
  • WhatsApp message logs: retained for 90 days from the date of sending, then permanently deleted.
  • Pixel event data: retained for 12 months on a rolling basis; events older than 12 months are purged automatically.
  • Staff account data: deleted within 30 days of account termination or Merchant subscription cancellation, whichever is sooner.
  • Contact form submissions: retained for 24 months or until the enquiry is resolved, whichever is later.

Merchants may request earlier deletion of their data by contacting us at the address below, subject to any conflicting statutory retention obligation.

Cookies & Tracking

The Antortiq Panel (antortiq.onrender.com) uses session-scoped authentication and does not place persistent tracking cookies on your browser.

The Antortiq Pixel is a JavaScript snippet that Merchants embed in their Shopify storefronts. It fires first-party events (ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase) and sends them to the Antortiq API. It does not use third-party cookies, does not fingerprint devices, and does not share data with advertising networks.

Event data captured by the Pixel is scoped to the Merchant whose storefront it is embedded on, and is used only for that Merchant's funnel analytics within the Antortiq Panel.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • API authentication via secret keys; demo panel access via rotating demo keys
  • HTTPS for all data in transit (TLS 1.2+)
  • MongoDB Atlas encryption at rest
  • Role-based access controls for Merchant staff accounts
  • No storage of payment card data on Antortiq infrastructure

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to adversely affect your rights, we will notify affected parties and the relevant authority as required by law.

Children

Antortiq's services are intended for businesses and their adult staff. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age. If a Merchant's end customer is a minor, responsibility for obtaining appropriate parental consent lies with the Merchant as Data Fiduciary.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.
